Nebbi B2B Sales Pipeline

Seven stages from first touchpoint to active partnership. Click any stage to expand. Turn on Edit mode to update text, add links, or add bullets.

1. Initial Outreach
2. Discovery Call
3. Post-Discovery Follow-Up
4. Pricing + Proposal
5. Negotiation + Alignment
6. Contract
7. Onboarding
1
Initial outreach
Intro email, warm intro, or inbound inquiry
Goal
  • Introduce Nebbi in a simple, approachable way
  • Establish relevance and interest
  • Gauge whether there is a potential fit worth exploring
Deliverables sent
  • Short "Who is Nebbi?" overview
  • What Nebbi does, who it serves, key outcomes
  • Call scheduling link or suggested meeting times
Communication
  • Intro email (sector-specific)
  • Follow-up email 5 days later if no reply
  • Final follow-up 7 days after that
We walk away knowing
  • Is there enough interest to continue?
  • What type of company or org are they?
  • Who is leading the conversation internally?
  • Are they actively looking or just exploring?
2
Discovery call
Learn their world before pitching ours
Goal
  • Learn what the company is trying to achieve
  • Understand pain points, employee goals, and current wellness structure
  • Begin identifying potential use cases and internal champions
Deliverables sent
  • Live overview and demo of Nebbi
  • Partnership deck (sector-specific)
  • 1-week free Nebbi trial access
  • Strategic brainstorm around possible implementation
Topics to cover
  • Employee engagement goals
  • Retention, culture initiative, 
  • Existing wellness offerings
  • Budget structure and decision-making process
  • Timeline for rollout
We walk away knowing
  • Does Nebbi genuinely fit their needs?
  • What "bucket" do they fall into?
  • Who are the key decision makers?
  • Where would budget come from?
  • What would success look like for them?
  • What is their employee size? 
  • Would initial on-boarding be full scale?
3
Post-discovery follow-up
Recap, position, and build momentum
Goal
  • Recap what was discussed
  • Position Nebbi as a strategic solution
  • Build confidence and momentum toward proposal
Deliverables sent
  • Nebbi implementation recommendation
    • Suggested service structure and rollout timeline
    • Goals and success metrics
Communication
  • Follow-up email with recap and recommendation outline
  • Optional strategy call to align on direction

We walk away knowing
  • Internal alignment on their side
  • Clarity around interest level and urgency
  • Buy-in from additional stakeholders
  • Understanding of timing and next decision gate
4
Pricing + proposal call
Present investment clearly, tied to outcomes
Goal
  • Walk through pricing clearly and confidently
  • Explain partnership structure and expectations
  • Tie pricing back to outcomes and value
Deliverables sent
  • Pricing proposal and package recommendation
    • Timeline overview
  • Optional tiers and add-ons if applicable
Topics to cover
  • Pricing Option
  • User counts and team size
  • Pilot vs. full launch options
  • Support level and contract structure
  • High Level Contract nods
We walk away knowing
  • Budget confirmation
  • Timeline confirmation
  • Remaining objections or questions
  • Clear next steps and who owns them
5
Negotiation + final alignment
Remove friction, reach green light
Goal
  • Finalize scope and terms
  • Remove friction from closing
  • Ensure both sides feel aligned
Deliverables sent
  • Revised proposal if needed
    • Final pricing and timeline confirmation
  • Clarified terms in writing
Communication
  • Call to resolve remaining objections
  • Follow-up email confirming verbal agreement
  • Confirm all final decision makers are aligned
  • Hint at on-boarding logistics and needs
We walk away knowing
  • Verbal agreement secured
  • All final decision makers aligned
  • Green light to move to contract
6
Contract
Close it cleanly, prepare them for onboarding
Goal
  • Officially secure the partnership
  • Keep momentum high
  • Prepare them internally for onboarding
Deliverables sent
  • Contract
  • Initial onboarding checklist preview
  • Reciprocal Marketing Expectations
  • Invoice issued
Internal materials they need ready
  • Employee count
  • Team and admin contacts
  • Branding assets if applicable
  • Internal communications contacts
  • Rollout dates
  • Wellness or benefits documentation
We walk away with
  • Signed agreement
  • Confirmed kickoff timeline
  • Primary point of contact established
  • Relationship handed to onboarding track
7
Onboarding
High-touch launch, set them up to succeed
Goal
  • Create a smooth, high-touch onboarding experience
  • Make implementation feel easy and organized
  • Build excitement internally
Deliverables sent
  • Onboarding steps PDF
  • Admin dashboard credentials
  • Portal walkthrough call
  • Launch checklist
  • Communication templates if applicable
Onboarding call includes
  • Portal walkthrough (15 min)
  • Admin setup
  • User experience overview
  • Launch strategy discussion
  • Set mid-program check-in date now
  • Q&A
We walk away with
  • Fully prepared client
  • Internal launch readiness confirmed
  • Confirmed implementation timeline
  • Activation target: 50%+ within 7 days
  • Strong relationship and confidence moving forward
